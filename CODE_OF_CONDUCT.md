# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone — regardless of age, ability, appearance, background, identity, or experience — will be treated with respect and kindness.

## Our values

- **Be kind** — Assume good intent. Questions aren't dumb; they're learning.
- **Be inclusive** — MultiController is for classrooms and learners of all ages. Make space for everyone.
- **Be respectful** — Disagree constructively. Focus on ideas, not people.
- **Give credit** — Acknowledge contributions. Contributions matter, whatever size.

## Our expectations

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Helping others learn and contribute
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Personal attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported by contacting the maintainer at matthewstephenroberts@msn.com.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. The maintainer will respond in a way deemed appropriate to the circumstances.

Community members asked to stop any inappropriate behavior are expected to comply immediately.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.
